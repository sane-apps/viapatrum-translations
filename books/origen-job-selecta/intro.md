Origen of Alexandria (c. 185–c. 254) was an early Christian theologian, ascetic and biblical scholar born at Alexandria of Christian parents and educated in the Catechetical School of Alexandria under Pantaenus and Clement, which he later headed. This work is a commentary with no named addressee. It offers selective remarks on verses from the Book of Job to explain difficult phrases, draw moral applications and defend Job's endurance against mistaken readings.

Little earlier Christian writing had treated Job in a sustained way, and much of Origen's work on Job survives only through later excerpts where the attribution is sometimes uncertain. In these selections Origen reads Job's trials as a spiritual contest in which the devil attacks through possessions, family, body and friends, while the friends speak words that are partly true but ill timed and weigh down the soul. Readers meet Job, Eliphaz, Bildad, Zophar, Elihu, Satan, Paul, David, Moses, Joshua son of Nun and the translator Theodotion. Job, Eliphaz, Elihu, God, Paul, David and Theodotion are quoted, the dragon of Job 40 to 41 is read as Satan working through heretical teachers, the teachings of Marcion, Valentinus and Basilides are argued against as slander of the Creator, and God's address to Job is read as boldness given after trial with doubled blessings answering doubled virtue.

To follow the commentary it helps to know how key words are rendered here: athlete for one who endures trial, endurance for perseverance under suffering, courage, judgment, boldness to speak before God, the dragon for Satan in Job 40 to 41, heretic for false teaching, trial, church members, pastured souls, night watches and morning star. Joshua is used where older usage says Jesus son of Nave, and Psalm numbers and book names follow the Greek Old Testament rather than the Hebrew, with citations from Deuteronomy, the Psalms, the Gospels and the Pauline epistles. An adamant means an unbreakable stone, an earnest means a pledge given now and fulfilled later, and the ruling part means the mind or governing center of the soul.
