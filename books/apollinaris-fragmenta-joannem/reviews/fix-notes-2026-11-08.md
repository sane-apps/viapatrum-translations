# Apollinaris on John: hand corrections, 8 Nov 2026 (Claude)
Found by the John commentary editors; each checked against the Greek source in translations/*_source.json.

- u03-rem-mid: "They were guarding the entrance of Phoenix and shouting welcome" -> "With palms they were escorting his entry and shouting welcome". Mistranslation: Greek Φοίνιξιν αὐτοῦ τὴν εἴσοδον δορυφοροῦντες = escorting his entry with palms (not 'the entrance of Phoenix').
- u06-open: "Christ said, 'my Lord and my God' (John 20:28)" -> "he proclaimed Christ his own Lord and God (John 20:28)". Mistranslation: Greek κύριόν τε καὶ θεὸν ἑαυτοῦ ἀνεῖπεν τὸν Χριστόν = he (Thomas) proclaimed Christ his Lord and God; the English gave Thomas's words to Christ.
- u05-rem-mid: "at the threshold of the matter.er." -> "at the threshold of the matter.". Typo: stray 'er.'
